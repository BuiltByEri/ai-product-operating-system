# Stage 4: Engineering Readiness Review

This artifact is fictional and demonstrates AAPOS Executive Communication.

## Executive Summary

Northstar should pursue a governed onboarding MVP that improves vendor intake clarity, status visibility, Operations exception handling, and review-routing transparency. The recommendation balances speed with control and avoids committing to full automation before Engineering and Risk discovery are complete.

## Business Problem

Vendor onboarding is creating friction across Sales, Operations, and Risk. The visible symptom is activation pressure, but the underlying problem includes incomplete submissions, repeated manual follow-up, unclear status, and inconsistent review routing.

## Product Intent

Create a clearer, status-aware onboarding flow that captures required vendor information once, routes review consistently based on defined risk signals, and gives Operations visibility into exceptions without removing human compliance oversight.

## VUED + Risk Summary

| Lens | Rating | Executive Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Value | High | Improves vendor experience, operational focus, and risk consistency. |
| Urgency | Near-Term | Stakeholder pressure is rising, but no hard external deadline is supplied. |
| Effort | Unknown | Engineering discovery is required before commitment. |
| Dependencies | High | Requires Risk criteria, data ownership, document constraints, and Operations workflow alignment. |
| Risk | High | Acting too quickly could weaken controls. Waiting preserves current friction and inconsistent review. |

## Engineering Readiness

Engineering has enough Product direction to begin discovery and refinement around guided intake, onboarding status, exception handling, and review-routing indicators. The team should not commit to delivery scope or dates until data ownership, audit requirements, and review criteria are confirmed.

## Delivery Strategy

1. Confirm Risk criteria and data ownership.
2. Refine guided checklist and status model.
3. Validate exception queue feasibility.
4. Implement MVP only after Engineering and Product confirm readiness.
5. Revisit deferred automation after MVP learning.

## Dependencies and Open Questions

- Risk criteria for human review routing.
- Vendor profile source of truth.
- Document metadata access.
- Audit requirements for status and review signals.
- Operations queue workflow.

## Deferred Work

- Automated low-risk approval.
- Rule configuration interface.
- Full lifecycle analytics.
- Vendor master profile consolidation.
- Broad platform redesign.

## Executive Recommendation

Approve Engineering discovery and MVP refinement for the governed onboarding flow. Do not approve full automation or full platform redesign at this stage.

## Decision Requested

Approve the MVP direction and authorize Engineering discovery for feasibility, dependencies, and readiness validation.

## Confidence Statement

Confidence is moderate. The Product direction is clear, but Engineering feasibility and Risk criteria must be confirmed before delivery commitment.

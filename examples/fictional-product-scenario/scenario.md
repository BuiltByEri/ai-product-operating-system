# Fictional Scenario

## Northstar Vendor Onboarding Platform

This scenario is fictional and exists only to demonstrate AAPOS.

## Ambiguous Business Problem

Northstar is a fictional B2B payments company. Vendor activation is becoming harder to manage as sales volume grows. New vendors begin onboarding but often abandon the process before activation. Operations teams manually chase missing information. Compliance review is inconsistent because risk signals arrive in different formats. Sales is asking Product to shorten activation time, while Risk and Operations are concerned that faster onboarding could increase exposure.

## Initial Request

Sales asks for a faster onboarding flow so vendors can activate sooner. Operations asks for fewer manual handoffs. Risk asks for more consistent review checkpoints. Leadership asks whether the company should prioritize onboarding speed, control quality, or operational relief first.

## Constraints

- Existing onboarding forms are fragmented.
- Some vendor data is submitted more than once.
- Compliance review requires human oversight for high-risk cases.
- Engineering has not estimated the work.
- No production metrics are provided in this fictional example.

## AAPOS Framing

The Product Owner must clarify the problem, apply VUED + Risk, recommend an MVP, preserve human review, and prepare Engineering for refinement without inventing outcomes or commitments.

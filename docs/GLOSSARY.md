# Glossary

## AAPOS

AI-Assisted Product Operating System.

## AI Assistance

Use of AI to draft, structure, compare, summarize, or translate product work under human review.

## Approval Record

A cross-stage artifact that records human gate outcomes, approvers, decisions, notes, assumptions, and open risks.

## Approved Product Recommendation

The Product-owned decision artifact that locks Product Intent, scope, MVP, tradeoffs, and success measures before Engineering planning.

## Discovery Summary

The Product Discovery artifact that records the problem, assumptions, risks, information gaps, and decision-changing questions.

## Engineering Refinement Package

The readable Engineering planning artifact that translates approved Product Intent into epics, stories, dependencies, risks, discovery items, and delivery strategy.

## Engineering Refinement Workbook Map

The structured planning map used to preserve implementation traceability across epics, stories, discovery items, dependencies, risks, and readiness.

## Engineering Readiness Review

The executive communication artifact that translates Product and Engineering planning into a decision-ready story.

## Human Gate

A review or approval boundary that must be completed by the accountable human owner before work advances.

## Product Intent

The approved product direction and outcome that Engineering planning must preserve.

## VUED + Risk

The AAPOS prioritization lens: Value, Urgency, Effort, Dependencies, and Risk.

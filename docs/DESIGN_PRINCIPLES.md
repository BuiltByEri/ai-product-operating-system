# AAPOS Design Principles

## Principle 1 - AI Accelerates. Humans Decide.

AI may assist with drafting, organizing, comparing, summarizing, and translating. Human owners make decisions and remain accountable.

## Principle 2 - Discovery Comes Before Commitment.

Ambiguous work must be understood before scope, delivery sequence, or stakeholder messaging is committed.

## Principle 3 - Product Intent Locks Before Engineering Planning.

Engineering planning begins only after the Product Owner approves Product Intent, MVP scope, out-of-scope boundaries, assumptions, tradeoffs, and success measures.

## Principle 4 - Product Drives Engineering.

Engineering planning translates the approved Product Recommendation into delivery-ready detail. It does not redefine Product priority or expand scope.

## Principle 5 - Translate, Do Not Transcribe.

Each artifact must be written for its audience. Engineering detail belongs in engineering artifacts. Executive communication must explain the decision in plain product and business language.

## Principle 6 - Every Artifact Serves One Audience.

Artifacts should not try to satisfy every audience at once. Mixed-audience artifacts must still be clear about what is summary, what is implementation detail, and what requires a decision.

## Principle 7 - Make Uncertainty and Dependencies Visible.

Unknowns, assumptions, open questions, constraints, dependencies, and risks must be visible before commitment.

## Principle 8 - Separate Mitigation, Discovery, MVP, and Full Solution.

Immediate mitigation, Engineering discovery, MVP delivery, and full solution work should not be collapsed into one vague recommendation.

## Principle 9 - Avoid False Precision.

AAPOS does not invent estimates, dates, technical designs, regulations, metrics, or stakeholder commitments. Unknown means unknown.

## Principle 10 - Keep the Framework Vendor-Neutral.

Reference skills may mention current tools, but AAPOS does not require a specific AI model, vendor, issue tracker, presentation tool, or delivery platform.
